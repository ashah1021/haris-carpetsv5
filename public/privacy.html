Privacy Policy

Last updated 7 September 2026

Hari's Carpets respects your privacy. This page explains what information we collect through our website, how we use it and your rights.

Who we are

Hari's Carpets

020 8570 6502 · sales@hariscarpets.co.uk

Hari's Carpets is the data controller for personal information collected through this website.

Information we collect

If you contact us through our website, we may collect your:

  • Name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number, if provided
  • Enquiry details and message

We use this information to respond to your enquiry, provide quotations and, where applicable, supply our products and services.

We do not sell your personal information or use it for third-party advertising.

How your information is handled

One company handles it besides us: Cloudflare, who host this website and store your enquiry until it is deleted. We read enquiries on the website itself and reply from our own email, so nobody else handles your message on the way to us.

Our website also displays content from third parties, including Google Maps, Google Fonts and Instagram. These services may process technical information, such as your IP address, when their content is loaded.

How long we keep it

We keep personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to deal with your enquiry, provide our services and maintain appropriate business records. Enquiries sent through the website are deleted automatically after twelve months, and you can ask us to delete yours sooner at any time.

Cookies and saved preferences

  • The public website does not intentionally use advertising or analytics cookies. The one cookie this site can set is for shop staff signing in to update it, and it only exists after signing in.
  • If you save favourite flooring shades using the heart feature, these are stored in your own browser and are not sent to Hari's Carpets. You can remove them by clearing your browser data.

Your information and your rights

You can ask us to access, correct or delete personal information we hold about you.

To make a request, email sales@hariscarpets.co.uk or call 020 8570 6502. Please provide enough information for us to identify your records. We will respond within one month. See deleting your information for what happens when you ask us to delete it.

If you have purchased from us, we may need to retain certain records for legal, tax, accounting or warranty purposes. Where this applies, we will retain only the information we are required to keep.

You can also complain to the Information Commissioner's Office (ICO) at ico.org.uk if you are unhappy with how we have handled your personal information.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page.
