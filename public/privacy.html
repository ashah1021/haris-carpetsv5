Privacy

This explains what happens to your information when you use this website. It is written to be read, not to be got past.

Who we are

Hari's Carpets, 267 Hanworth Road, Hounslow, Middlesex, TW3 3RZ.
sales@hariscarpets.co.uk · 020 8570 6502

We are the data controller for the information described below.

What we collect, and only when you send it

The only information we ask for is on the enquiry form: your name, your email address, your phone number if you give one, what you are asking about, and your message. Nothing is collected if you simply read the site.

We use it to answer your enquiry and to quote for the work. In data protection terms that is our legitimate interest in responding to someone who has contacted us, and, where you go on to order flooring, taking steps to enter into a contract with you.

Who else handles it

  • Resend delivers the email to our inbox.
  • Cloudflare hosts the website and stores it.

Both act on our instructions and do not use your enquiry for anything of their own.

How long we keep it

Your enquiry sits in our mailbox for as long as we need it to deal with you properly, and afterwards for our own business records.

A separate copy is written to our storage before the email is sent, so that an enquiry is never lost if email delivery fails. That copy is deleted automatically 30 days after it is sent, or 90 days if it never sent. It is a safety net, not an archive.

Your browser

  • Shades you save with the ♥ are kept in your own browser and never sent to us. Clearing your browser data removes them.
  • We set no cookies on visitors. The one cookie this site can set is for shop staff signing in to update the site, and it only exists after signing in.
  • There is no analytics, no advertising pixel and no third-party tracking on this site.

Things on the page that come from elsewhere

Some parts of the page are loaded from other companies' servers. When that happens, those companies can see your IP address, because that is how the internet delivers the file to you. We do not send them anything about you.

  • Google Fonts — the typefaces, on every page.
  • Google Maps — the map, on the Visit us page only.
  • Instagram (Meta) — our recent posts on the home page. We read our own public posts; we are not given anything about you.

Your rights

You can ask us for a copy of what we hold about you, to correct it, to delete it, or to stop using it. Email or ring us using the details above and we will deal with it within one month.

To have your information deleted, email sales@hariscarpets.co.uk with the subject "Delete my information", or ring 020 8570 6502. See deleting your information for what happens next.

If you are not happy with how we have handled it you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

Changes

If this changes we will update this page and the date at the top.
