Deleting your information

Last updated 17 August 2026

How to ask

Email sales@hariscarpets.co.uk with the subject "Delete my information", or ring 020 8570 6502. Tell us the name, email address or phone number you used, so we can find it.

You do not need an account and there is nothing to sign in to. We will confirm by reply once it is done, within one month and usually the same week.

What gets deleted

  • Your enquiry. It is deleted anyway once we have dealt with you, and automatically after twelve months.
  • Your name, email address, phone number and message.
  • Any notes we made while quoting for the work.

What we may have to keep

If you have bought flooring from us, we have to keep the sale records HMRC requires, and enough to honour a guarantee. We will tell you if that applies to you, and delete everything else.

If you have not contacted us

There is nothing to delete. Reading this website creates no record of you: no account, no cookie, no analytics. Shades you save with the ♥ are kept in your own browser and never reach us — clearing your browser data removes them.

Instagram

Our home page shows our own recent Instagram posts. We are not given any information about people who view them. Anything Instagram itself holds about you is under your control in the Instagram app, not ours.
